# Appendix A — annotation codebook

Coders tick each construct the writer **actually expresses** about this stay.
Do not infer what the writer probably felt. A construct counts as present even
when phrased negatively. Several constructs may be present at once. Flag the
document instead of coding if it is not English, empty, or an automated message.

| Code | Construct | Tick present when the writer… |
|---|---|---|
| ATT | Attitude | evaluates the stay itself as good or bad |
| SN | Subjective norm | refers to other people, recommending the place, or citing friends, family or other reviewers |
| PBC | Perceived behavioural control | describes how easy or hard it was to book, arrive, communicate or manage the stay |
| SAT | Satisfaction | says the stay met, exceeded or fell short of expectations |
| BI | Revisit intention | states an intention or wish to come back, or explicitly rules it out |

This is a presence task, not a valence-only task. Recommending a listing to others
is not the same thing as perceived social pressure to revisit it.
